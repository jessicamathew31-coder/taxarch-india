## Rebate Under Section 87A

Old Regime: Up to Rs 12,500 if taxable income does not exceed Rs 5,00,000.
New Regime: Up to Rs 60,000 if taxable income does not exceed Rs 12,00,000 (effective FY 2025-26,
Finance Act 2024). This effectively makes the new regime tax-free up to Rs 12 lakh.

Rebate cannot exceed basic tax liability.

## Surcharge

| Gross Total Income | Rate (Old) | Rate (New) |
|-------------------|-----------|-----------|
| Up to Rs 50,00,000 | Nil | Nil |
| Rs 50,00,001 to Rs 1,00,00,000 | 10% | 10% |
| Rs 1,00,00,001 to Rs 2,00,00,000 | 15% | 15% |
| Rs 2,00,00,001 to Rs 5,00,00,000 | 25% | 25% |
| Above Rs 5,00,00,000 | 37% | 25% (capped) |

Surcharge is applied on basic tax (post rebate). Marginal relief applies at each threshold.

## Cess

Health and Education Cess: 4% on (basic tax after rebate + surcharge).

## Standard Deduction

Old regime: Rs 50,000 (salaried individuals and pensioners).
New regime: Rs 75,000 (enhanced from FY 2025-26, Finance Act 2024).

## Key Deductions (Old Regime Only Unless Noted)

### Section 80C (Aggregate ceiling Rs 1,50,000)

Includes: PPF, ELSS, NSC, life insurance premium, home loan principal repayment, 5-year tax
saving FD, NPS Tier 1 employee contribution, SSY, ULIP, tuition fees for two children.

### Section 80CCC

Contribution to pension fund of a life insurer. Counted within the Rs 1.5 lakh aggregate.

### Section 80CCD(1)

Employee or self-employed NPS Tier 1 contribution. Counted within the Rs 1.5 lakh aggregate.

### Section 80CCD(1B)

Additional NPS contribution. Up to Rs 50,000. Independent of the Rs 1.5 lakh aggregate.
This is the most commonly missed deduction in India. Maximum combined benefit from
80CCD(1) and 80CCD(1B) for an individual is Rs 2,00,000 (Rs 1.5L within 80C + Rs 50K separate).

### Section 80CCD(2)

Employer NPS contribution. Up to 10% of salary (basic + DA) for private sector employees,
14% for central government employees. Available under the new regime as well.

### Section 80D

Health insurance premium:
- Self, spouse, dependent children: up to Rs 25,000
- Parents: up to Rs 25,000 additional (Rs 50,000 if senior citizens)
- Preventive health check-up: up to Rs 5,000 within the above limits

### Section 80E

Interest on education loan for higher studies. No upper limit. Available for 8 consecutive
assessment years from the year repayment begins.

### Section 24(b) — Home Loan Interest

Self-occupied property: up to Rs 2,00,000 per year. Acquisition/construction must be
completed within 5 years from the end of the financial year in which loan was taken.
Let-out property: full interest deductible (no cap).

### HRA Exemption

Minimum of:
1. Actual HRA received from employer
2. 50% of basic salary (metro cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai) or 40% (others)
3. Rent paid minus 10% of basic salary

### Section 80TTA

Interest from savings bank accounts. Up to Rs 10,000. For individuals below 60 years.

### Section 80TTB

Interest from all deposits (savings and FD). Up to Rs 50,000. Only for senior citizens (60+).
Replaces 80TTA for senior citizens.

### Professional Tax

Deductible from gross salary. Maximum Rs 2,500 per year. State-levied.

## New Regime: Permissible Deductions

The new regime disallows most deductions. Only the following are available:

- Standard deduction: Rs 75,000
- Section 80CCD(2): Employer NPS contribution
- Section 80JJAA: Deduction for new employment (for employers)
- Agniveer Corpus Fund contribution (Section 80CCH)

All other Chapter VI-A deductions (80C, 80D, 80E, 80G, 80TTA etc.) are not available.
HRA exemption and LTA exemption are also not available under the new regime.

## Investment Instrument Returns (FY 2025-26)

| Instrument | Rate | Tax Treatment | Lock-in |
|-----------|------|---------------|---------|
| PPF | 7.1% | EEE (fully exempt) | 15 years |
| NSC | 7.7% | Taxable on maturity | 5 years |
| SSY | 8.2% | EEE | 21 years |
| ELSS | ~12% (market) | LTCG at 12.5% above Rs 1.25L | 3 years |
| NPS Tier 1 | ~10% (E50) | 60% exempt, 40% annuity | Till 60 |
| Tax-saving FD | ~6.8% | Taxable as income | 5 years |
| LIC endowment | ~5.5% | Exempt if premium < 10% of SA | Policy term |
| ULIP | ~9% | Exempt if premium < Rs 2.5L/yr | 5 years |

PPF, NSC, SSY rates are government-notified for Q1 FY 2025-26.
ELSS return is the long-run average; actual returns vary significantly year to year.
NPS return assumes a moderate E50 (50% equity) allocation.
